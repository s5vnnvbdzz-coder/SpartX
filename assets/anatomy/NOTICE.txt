SpartanX anatomical muscle figures (front/back male/female)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Anatomical vector paths and body contours are adapted from:
  https://github.com/NextSolutionsStudio/anatome
  Apache License, Version 2.0
  https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

The upstream Anatome project credits the muscle path system to
HichamELBSI/react-native-body-highlighter. SpartanX uses its own DOM/SVG renderer,
theme styling, primary/secondary selection and data integration.

Exercise thumbnail illustrations are served in-app from the RepDB free dataset:
  https://github.com/RepDB/exercise-dataset
  https://repdb.co
These exercise images are licensed by RepDB for commercial/personal in-app use
with visible attribution. Their dataset may not be redistributed as a dataset.
The images are not used for generative AI training or transformation.

The original SpartanX exercise names, workout logs, ranking and muscle
classification data remain separate from these image assets. No third-party
user-specific workout data is sent to these providers.
