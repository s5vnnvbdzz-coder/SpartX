Know what you've trained.

UNDERSTANDING THIS VIEW

What this does — and doesn't — mean

Counts are assigned to the primary muscle group of each recognised exercise. Compound movements also recruit other muscles, so this is a simplified workload view. Recovery depends on sleep, effort, nutrition and individual variation; SpartanX does not diagnose injury or determine whether a muscle is fully recovered.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS

Intelligence is a decision aid, not a coach or clinician.

Recommendations currently use recent top sets, repetitions, estimated strength trends and logged RPE. They cannot assess bar speed, technique, sleep quality, pain, injury risk or actual muscle recovery. Use judgement and adapt training to how you feel. This module does not write to SpartanX or change any workout.
